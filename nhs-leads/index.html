FAQ

How many NHS leads will I receive?+

Approximately 500 leads a day. The exact number can be higher or lower depending on live hiring activity and the vacancies available across the market.

What counts as a lead?+

A lead is an active NHS vacancy with useful prospecting data such as the hiring organisation, job title, location and a relevant decision-maker or business email where available.

Where do the leads come from?+

We gather active vacancies from multiple sources around the internet, including job boards and individual careers pages, then bring them together into one structured feed.

How are the leads delivered?+

They are delivered in an organised Google Sheet that continuously grows as fresh leads are added, so your recruiters can filter by role, location or organisation and work from one shared source.

Are decision-maker emails included?+

Yes, relevant decision-maker or business emails are included where they can be identified. Availability can vary by organisation and vacancy.

How much does the service cost?+

Pricing depends on the volume, delivery setup and how your team plans to use the data. Get in touch and we’ll recommend the right arrangement.
