FAQs

A few things
you might ask.

How quickly will I receive CVs?+

Most roles are delivered within 1 hour. Harder-to-fill or more niche roles can take a little longer, depending on the brief and candidate market.

How many CVs do I receive per vacancy?+

Your plan includes 12–15 vetted, relevant CVs each day.

Are the CVs vetted?+

Yes. Every CV is rigorously checked against your brief, including skills, experience, location and the recency of the profile where available. Weak, outdated or irrelevant matches are removed so only the strongest CVs reach your shortlist.

Do I need my own CV database subscription?+

No. The point is to give your agency a sourcing function without requiring your recruiters to spend their day searching a CV database.

What's the catch? Is it too good to be true?+

There isn't one. Our sourcers use multiple leading CV databases and online sourcing channels, then handle the searching, filtering and vetting for you. You get access to that sourcing capability through one simple monthly subscription, without stacking up separate database licences that can run into four figures every month. Less cost, less admin and far less work for your recruiters.

Can I send more than one vacancy?+

Yes. Send us the vacancies you want worked and we’ll source up to your daily CV allowance.
